# Conference and institution marks

Checked 13 August 2026. Marks are stored locally so the published page does not hotlink external assets.

- `../bmvc-2026.png` — resized, otherwise unmodified derivative of the [official BMVC 2026 transparent mark](https://bmvc2026.bmva.org/assets/images/layout/bmvc-2026-transparent.png), linked to the [BMVC 2026 website](https://bmvc2026.bmva.org/).
- `university-of-liverpool.svg` — the University of Liverpool header wordmark extracted without geometric alteration from the [official university website](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/); its inherited official header colour is fixed to `#212b58` for standalone rendering.
- `university-of-exeter.svg` — [official University of Exeter green mark](https://www.exeter.ac.uk/v8media/global/images/UOE-green.svg), selected for legibility on the light institutional field.
- `university-of-birmingham.svg` — [official University of Birmingham light-background mark](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/Images/logos/uob/uob-uk-logo-light.svg).

Placement is labelled “Organising institutions” and denotes committee affiliation, not sponsorship or endorsement. Before public launch, organisers should confirm that each university permits this use and replace these working assets with any brand-team supplied files. Do not crop, recolour, distort, rearrange, or add effects to the marks.
